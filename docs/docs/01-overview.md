# Overview

Welcome to [**Glows.ai**](https://glows.ai/), a one-stop AI platform that helps you easily handle AI training & inference, large-scale data processing, and multi-node collaboration. This tutorial will guide you through the platform's core features and advanced operations, so you can flexibly build the tools and services you need.

---

## **Platform Highlights**

1. **Compute Management**
   Quickly create and monitor instances, efficiently configuring GPU and CPU resources to handle a wide range of needs.
2. **Ready to Go**
   A full suite of mainstream tool images — just launch an instance and start using them, skipping tedious installation steps.
3. **Data Security**
   Simplify your workflow with snapshot and backup features, reducing the risk of accidental deletion.
4. **Storage Optimization**
   Manage your storage space to improve resource efficiency.
5. **Cost Transparency**
   A clear billing system to help you track spending and configure the lowest-cost setup.
6. **Glows.ai Team**
   A resource-sharing management platform designed for developer teams.

---

## **What You'll Learn**

In this tutorial, you'll explore the following core features:

- **Get Started**: Register an account and complete basic setup to quickly get familiar with the platform.
- **Create New**: Learn how to configure the GPU and CPU that fit your project's needs, tailoring the ideal compute system.
- **My Instance**: Manage all your instances and monitor their running status.
- **Datadrive**: Freely upload data and flexibly allocate it to instances, and learn how to manage data across different instances.
- **Snapshots**: Reduce repetitive setup steps and streamline your workflow with instant instance snapshots and convenient restoration.
- **Images**: Learn about the variety of images available across different machine types, and how to upload your own custom images.
- **Space Management**: Flexibly allocate different sections of your storage space to match different stages of development.
- **Auto Deploy**: Configure an automated startup/shutdown process so your machine doesn't burn through your budget while idle.
- **Glows.ai Team**: Build a compute management environment your team can pick up quickly, sharing resources securely and efficiently.
- **Mesh Clustering**: Configure multi-node Mesh Clustering for efficient collaboration and resource allocation.
- **Billing**: View your account balance and detailed billing information.
- **User Profile**: Adjust your user settings for a better experience.

---

## **Learning Outcomes**

By the end of this tutorial, you'll be able to:

- Independently use [**Glows.ai**](https://glows.ai/) to create and manage instances, applying its core features.
- Effectively protect your data and master multi-node collaboration techniques.
- Optimize cost and storage management to improve efficiency and streamline your workflow.

Whether you're a developer, a business manager, or part of an educational institution, **Glows.AI** can be an essential partner for your projects and services.
